Wren Support

Offline pregnancy food scanner for iPhone.

Need help? Email support@madebykamal.app. Every message is read and answered by the developer, usually within a couple of days.

Common questions

Why does Wren never tell me a food is safe?

Because it cannot know. A printed label cannot rule out how a food was stored, whether it was reheated properly, or what happened on shared equipment. Wren tells you what the published guidance says about the ingredients it found, names the agency it came from, and leaves the decision with you and your clinician. That is a deliberate design rule and it is enforced by an automated check that fails the build if the word appears in pregnancy-related screen text.

Why did it say it could not read the label?

Wren checks whether the text it recovered actually looks like an ingredient list. If the photo is blurry, angled, or the print is very small, the reader can return confident nonsense. Rather than run pregnancy checks over nonsense and report nothing found, Wren tells you it could not read it. Try again with more light and the panel flat to the camera.

Where do the fish recommendations come from?

The fish guide covers 62 species and follows the FDA and EPA joint advice about eating fish for people who are pregnant or breastfeeding. Each entry names its category and links the advice it came from, so you can read the source rather than take our word for it.

How is the caffeine limit worked out?

Wren uses the reference figure published by ACOG for pregnancy and shows how the drinks and foods you log add up against it. Caffeine content varies a great deal between brands and brews, so the app shows a reference amount rather than a measurement of the cup in front of you.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The product database and every pregnancy rule are stored on your device. Wren makes no network requests at all, apart from Apple handling a purchase if you buy a pass.

Something here is wrong about a product

See Corrections. Manufacturers and shoppers can both report an error, and it will be fixed in the next data build.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Settings inside Wren and tap Restore Purchases. The pregnancy checks were never behind the paywall and keep working either way.
