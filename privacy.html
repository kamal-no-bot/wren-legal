Privacy Policy

The short version: Wren collects nothing, because it sends nothing anywhere.

What Wren collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting service.

What stays on your device

  • Your pregnancy profile, including your trimester if you set one.
  • Anything you log in the caffeine planner.
  • Your scan history.
  • Whether you have bought a pass, and whether you have left a tip.

All of it is stored in the app container on your iPhone. None of it is transmitted, and the developer cannot see any of it.

This is health information, and it never leaves your phone

A pregnancy profile is sensitive in a way an ingredient preference is not. Wren treats it accordingly: it is not synced, not backed up to any server we control, not shared with any third party, and not used to target anything at you. It exists so the app can tailor what it shows you, and for no other purpose. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera

Wren reads barcodes and ingredient panels using the camera. Frames are processed on the device by Apple frameworks and are never written to your photo library, never stored by the app, and never uploaded. Wren asks for camera access only when you open the scanning screen.

Network

The app target contains no networking code. This is enforced automatically: the build fails if a networking symbol appears anywhere in it. The only network activity is Apple handling a purchase or restore if you choose to buy a pass, which is Apple sending Apple your purchase, not Wren sending anything to us.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Wren never sees your payment details. Apple provides the app with a receipt confirming the purchase, which stays on your device.

Children

Wren is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will appear here and in the app release notes. Any version of Wren that starts sending data anywhere would be a different app to the one described here.

Wren is made by an independent developer. Last updated 24 August 2026.
